Platform Features

Disaster Response Capabilities

Core tools for emergency reporting, volunteer coordination, and resource management.

🚨

Emergency Request Management

Citizens can submit emergency requests with their name, contact number, location, disaster type, urgency level, and description.

🤝

Volunteer Coordination

Volunteers can register their skills, availability, location, and contact details for coordinated disaster response.

📦

Resource Management

Emergency resources can be added, monitored, updated, and managed according to current availability and operational status.

📋

Emergency Status Tracking

Coordinators can monitor emergency requests and update their status as they move through the response process.

🛡

Volunteer Assignment

Coordinators can review volunteers and assign suitable responders to emergency requests.

📊

Operational Dashboard

A centralized dashboard provides coordinators with emergency, volunteer, resource, and notification information in one place.
